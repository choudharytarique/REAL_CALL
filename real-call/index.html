About Real Call

Real Call is an English-learning platform designed to help people improve spoken English through real practice.

We offer:

  • 1-on-1 Real Call — live audio-only conversations with other learners (WebRTC). No video.
  • AI Voice Call — conversation practice with AI characters and grammar feedback (when an AI service is available).

  • Free English lessons — original educational content you can read and practise any time.

Our goal is to make spoken English practice accessible, useful, and safe. Partner learners and AI characters are for practice only; they are not professional teachers or the real celebrities some characters are named after.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Real Call handles information when you use the website and app.

1. Information stored on your device

Most of your learning data stays in your browser’s local storage, including:

  • Display name and English level preference
  • Settings (theme, learning mode, speech rate, etc.)
  • XP, game scores, completed activities, and recent session history
  • Local account credentials if you create an email-based account on this device (password is hashed in the browser for local use)

We do not operate a permanent user database on our servers for this learning data. Clearing site data in your browser or using “Delete all data” in Settings removes it from that device.

2. Real-person voice calls

1-on-1 calls use WebRTC for audio between two browsers. Signalling (matchmaking and connection setup) is handled through our server over WebSocket while a call is being arranged or active. We do not record or store the audio of your conversations on our servers. Call audio is streamed peer-to-peer (or via standard WebRTC paths) for the duration of the call only.

3. Microphone and speech recognition

Microphone access is requested only when you start a voice feature. If your browser uses a speech-recognition service, that service may process audio according to the browser provider’s policies. We do not receive raw call audio for storage.

4. AI conversation practice

When AI Voice is available, the text of the conversation may be sent to the configured AI service (for example, a local Ollama instance or a service the operator has set up) to generate replies. This happens only when you use the AI feature.

5. Google Sign-In

If you choose “Continue with Google” and Google Sign-In is configured, Google’s authentication service processes your sign-in according to Google’s policies. We receive basic profile information needed to identify the session on this device. Configure Google OAuth only with your own Client ID; do not put secrets in frontend code.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

See our . We use essential local storage for app function. We do not currently load third-party advertising cookies. If advertising or analytics is added later, this policy will be updated and, where required, consent will be requested.

7. Your choices

  • Export your data from Settings
  • Clear history, reset progress, or delete all local data
  • Contact us to request assistance with privacy questions

Help & FAQ

How do 1v1 Real Calls work?

Both users open Real Call, choose a topic, and press Start. When two people are searching for the same topic (or compatible queue), the server matches them and WebRTC connects audio. Allow microphone permission. Calls are audio-only.

Why can’t I hear the other person?

Check microphone permission, speaker control, and network. Some strict firewalls need a TURN server (optional; configure in code if required). Try Chrome or Edge. Both users must be connected to the same Real Call server.

Does AI work without internet?

AI replies need the configured AI service (for example Ollama on the server). Games and local lessons can still be used offline in many cases. Google Login and live peer calls need internet.

Where is my progress stored?

In your browser’s local storage on that device. Export or delete it from Settings.

How do I report abuse?

Email mrt707174@gmail.com with a short description. See Safety and Community Guidelines.
